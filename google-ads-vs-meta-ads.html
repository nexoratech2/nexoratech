Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Which Should You Start With?

5 min read · Nexora Tech Team

Both platforms can work well — but they work in fundamentally different ways, and starting with the wrong one for your business wastes budget fast.

How they're fundamentally different

Google Ads captures demand that already exists — someone is actively searching for what you offer, right now. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) creates demand by putting your offer in front of people who weren't necessarily looking for it, based on their interests and behavior.

When Google Ads makes more sense

  • Your product or service is something people actively search for (a plumber, a specific product, a solution to a known problem)
  • You want higher-intent traffic, even at a higher cost per click
  • Your business is local and search intent is strong ("near me" searches)

When Meta Ads makes more sense

  • Your product is visual and benefits from being seen, not searched for
  • You're building awareness for something new that people don't yet know to search for
  • You have strong photo or video content to work with
Google Ads meets people where they already are. Meta Ads introduces you to people who don't know you yet.

Budget considerations

Google Ads often costs more per click but converts at higher intent, which can make it more efficient for high-value purchases. Meta Ads typically costs less per click but requires stronger creative and more patience to find what resonates.

Our honest recommendation

If your product or service has clear, existing search demand, start with Google Ads. If you're building brand awareness for something newer or highly visual, start with Meta. Many businesses eventually use both — but starting with the one that matches your actual sales motion avoids wasted early budget.
