How Much Does a Shopify Store Really Cost to Build?

By Afolabi Oluwaseyi Christopher · July 2026 · 5 min read
A professionally built Shopify store typically costs between $500 and $5,000+ depending on complexity, plus a recurring Shopify subscription starting around $39/month — DIY templates land at the low end, custom builds at the high end.

The two separate costs people confuse

There's the cost of building the store (a one-time project cost) and the cost of running it (an ongoing monthly cost). Most people asking "how much does Shopify cost" are really asking about the build, so let's start there.

Build cost: what you're actually paying for

A basic store using a free or low-cost theme with minimal customization can run $300–$800 if you hire someone, or be free if you do it entirely yourself with default settings. A more customized store — unique design, product photography integration, custom sections, app configuration — typically lands between $800 and $2,500. A fully custom storefront with bespoke design, advanced functionality, and conversion optimization can run $2,500–$5,000+.

Where the price actually goes: theme customization or custom coding, setting up your product catalog properly, configuring shipping and tax rules correctly for your region, connecting payment processors, and building out pages like About, FAQ, and policies that customers actually trust.

Running cost: the part people forget

  • Shopify subscription: plans start around $39/month for the Basic tier
  • Apps: most stores add 2-5 paid apps (reviews, email marketing, upsells) totaling $20-$100/month
  • Domain name: roughly $10-15/year
  • Transaction fees: avoided if you use Shopify Payments, otherwise 0.5-2% extra per sale

Where people overspend — and underspend

The most common overspend is paying for a fully custom build when a well-customized template would've served the business just as well in year one. The most common underspend is skipping proper product photography and copywriting — a beautifully coded store with poor product presentation still won't convert.

Is Shopify's free trial enough to build a full store?

It's enough to build and test, but you'll need a paid plan before you can actually accept live payments and launch.

Does a more expensive store guarantee more sales?

No — a $5,000 store with weak product-market fit will underperform a $500 store selling something people actually want. Design supports sales; it doesn't replace demand.
