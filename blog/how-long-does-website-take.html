How Long Does It Take to Build a Website?

By Afolabi Oluwaseyi Christopher · July 2026 · 4 min read
A simple website typically takes 1-2 weeks, a standard business website 2-4 weeks, and a full e-commerce store 3-8 weeks — the biggest factor affecting timeline is usually how quickly the client provides content and feedback, not the actual build work.

Typical timelines by project type

  • Simple landing page (1-3 pages): 1-2 weeks
  • Standard business website (5-10 pages): 2-4 weeks
  • E-commerce store: 3-8 weeks depending on catalog size and custom functionality
  • Complex custom platform (booking systems, memberships): 8+ weeks

The real bottleneck is usually content, not code

Designers and developers can often build faster than clients can provide the content that build actually needs — final copy, approved photos, logo files, product information. Projects that stay on the shorter end of these timelines are almost always ones where the client had content ready upfront.

What actually extends a timeline

  • Waiting on content, photos, or copy from the client
  • Multiple rounds of significant design changes after initial approval
  • Adding new features or pages mid-project that weren't in the original scope
  • Slow feedback turnaround between revision rounds

How to keep your project on the shorter end

Have your core content — logo, key photos, service descriptions, testimonials if you have them — ready before the project starts, and aim to turn around feedback within a day or two of each revision round rather than letting it sit.

Is a faster timeline always better?

Not necessarily — a rushed build sometimes skips proper testing, SEO setup, or content refinement. A realistic timeline that includes time for a proper review before launch tends to produce a better long-term result than the absolute fastest possible turnaround.

Can a website be built in a single day?

A very basic one-page site using a template, yes — but it won't include the customization, SEO setup, or content refinement a proper business site needs.

What's the single biggest thing I can do to speed up my project?

Have your content ready before the project starts, and respond to revision requests quickly — this alone often cuts timelines by weeks.
