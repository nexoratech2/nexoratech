How to Choose the Right Web Designer for Your Business

6 min read · Nexora Tech Team

The gap between a great web designer and a mediocre one shows up for years after the project ends. Here's how to tell the difference before you hire.

Look at their portfolio critically

Don't just check if the work looks polished — check if it looks varied. A portfolio full of near-identical templates suggests a designer who reuses one formula regardless of the client's actual needs.

Ask about process, not just price

A designer who can clearly explain how they'll approach your project — discovery, wireframes, revisions, launch — is far more likely to deliver a smooth experience than one who jumps straight to a quote with no process behind it.

Check for business thinking, not just visual design

The best web designers ask about your customers and your goals before they ask about your color preferences. A site that looks good but doesn't consider how visitors actually convert is a missed opportunity.

You're not hiring someone to make a website. You're hiring someone to solve a business problem that happens to involve a website.

Read between the lines of reviews and references

Past clients will tell you what the portfolio can't: whether deadlines were met, whether communication was clear, and whether the final result matched what was promised.

Clarify what happens after launch

Ask directly: who updates the site after it's live? Is there a maintenance plan, or are you on your own? This single question avoids a huge amount of frustration down the line.

Red flags to watch for

  • No clear contract or scope of work
  • Pressure to decide immediately
  • Reluctance to share past client references
  • A price that seems too good to be true relative to the scope
