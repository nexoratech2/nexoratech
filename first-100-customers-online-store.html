How to Get Your First 100 Customers for a New Online Store

6 min read · Nexora Tech Team

The first 100 customers are the hardest to get and the most important — they're what everything after them is built on.

Start with people who already know you

Before any paid strategy, your existing network — personal contacts, social followers, past clients from other ventures — is the fastest, cheapest source of first customers, and their word of mouth carries more weight than a stranger's ad.

Get real reviews as early as possible

A store with zero reviews asks new visitors to take a leap of faith. Actively asking your first customers for a review (and making it easy to leave one) compounds — each review makes the next sale a little easier to earn.

Use organic social before paid ads

Paid ads amplify what's already working; they rarely fix a message that isn't resonating yet. Testing your offer and messaging organically first — even with a small following — tells you what's worth paying to amplify later.

The first 100 customers aren't won with a bigger budget. They're won with more direct, personal effort than feels scalable.

Consider a launch offer

A limited-time discount or bonus for early customers lowers the risk of being a first-time buyer and creates a real reason to act now instead of "maybe later."

Go where your specific customer already spends time

Relevant online communities, local groups, or niche social spaces where your ideal customer already gathers are often far more effective early on than broad advertising to strangers.

Track what's actually working

Ask early customers how they found you. In the first 100 sales, this simple habit tells you more about where to focus than any amount of guessing.
