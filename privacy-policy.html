Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Nexora Tech ("we," "us," "our") collects when you visit nexoratech2.github.io/nexoratech, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect limited information through the following:

  • Contact form submissions: your name, email, service interest, budget range, and message, when you voluntarily submit our contact form.
  • Basic site analytics: with your consent (via our cookie banner), we log your approximate country (derived from your IP address, not your precise location) and which pages/buttons you interact with, to understand overall site traffic. We do not collect or store your IP address itself, name, or any other personally identifying detail through this analytics system.
  • Live chat: if you use our chat widget (powered by Tawk.to), any messages you send are processed by Tawk.to's own systems. See their privacy policy for details on how they handle that data.
  • Cookies: we use a small number of cookies/local storage entries to remember your cookie consent choice and to support the live chat widget.

How we use this information

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To understand which pages and countries our visitors come from, so we can improve the site
  • To operate the live chat feature, if you choose to use it

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

  • Tawk.to — live chat widget
  • Google Sheets / Google Apps Script — used to store contact form submissions and aggregate anonymous visit statistics
  • Google Analytics — site traffic analytics (once enabled)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on their end.

Your choices

You can decline non-essential cookies at any time via the cookie banner shown on your first visit. Declining means analytics logging and live chat will not load. You can also clear your browser's cookies/local storage at any time to reset your saved preference.

Data retention

Contact form submissions and analytics data are retained in our Google Sheets for as long as reasonably needed for business purposes. You can request deletion of any personal information you've submitted by contacting us directly (details below).

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business use and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
