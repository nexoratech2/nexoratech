Squarespace vs WordPress: Which Should You Choose?

By Afolabi Oluwaseyi Christopher · July 2026 · 5 min read
Squarespace wins for design-forward businesses (photographers, creatives, restaurants) who want a beautiful site with minimal setup; WordPress wins for anyone who needs deep customization, complex functionality, or maximum long-term control.

Design quality out of the box

Squarespace is well known for genuinely beautiful, design-forward templates that look professional with minimal tweaking — this is its strongest selling point. WordPress themes vary wildly in quality; a great-looking WordPress site usually requires either a premium theme or custom design work.

Ease of use

Squarespace's editor is intuitive and contained — you can't easily break your site by clicking the wrong thing. WordPress gives you (and any collaborator) far more room to customize, but also more room to make a mess of things without some know-how.

Flexibility and functionality

This is where WordPress pulls ahead decisively. Its plugin ecosystem covers nearly anything — membership sites, complex booking systems, multi-vendor marketplaces, advanced SEO tools. Squarespace covers the common cases well but hits a ceiling faster if your needs get specific or unusual.

Cost

Squarespace is a flat monthly fee (roughly $16-$49/month) with hosting included — simple and predictable. WordPress costs vary more (hosting, theme, plugins) but can be cheaper or more expensive depending on your choices, with more moving parts to manage.

The deciding question

If your business is fundamentally visual and simple (portfolio, restaurant, event, personal brand), Squarespace's design quality and simplicity make it hard to beat. If you need specific functionality beyond a standard business site — or expect to keep adding features for years — WordPress's flexibility is worth the extra setup complexity.

Is Squarespace good for SEO?

Squarespace has improved a lot here and handles the basics well, but WordPress still offers more granular SEO control for businesses where search ranking is a primary growth channel.

Can Squarespace handle an online store?

Yes, for small to medium product catalogs. For serious e-commerce with complex inventory, most businesses still lean toward Shopify or WooCommerce on WordPress.
